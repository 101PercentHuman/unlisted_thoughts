Heraldry is one of the four pillars of humanity—right behind public administration, secularisation of universities, and physics.

Unlisted Thoughts has an **Unlisted Herald**, who is responsible, under the [*Heraldry By-law 2026*](/files/bylaws/Heraldry_By-law_2026.pdf), for:
<div class="legal-list">
  <p class="legal-item">
    <span class="legal-label">(a)</span>
    deal with heraldry in the association with due regard also to vexillology and genealogy;
  </p>
  <p class="legal-item">
    <span class="legal-label">(b)</span>
    advise the Board on the granting of Arms;
  </p>
  <p class="legal-item">
    <span class="legal-label">(c)</span>
    maintain a register known as the <em>Register of Unlisted Arms</em>;
  </p>
  <p class="legal-item">
    <span class="legal-label">(d)</span>
    establish and maintain other such registers, inventories, and indices as may be required in connection with the good maintenance of the Register of Unlisted Arms;
  </p>
  <p class="legal-item">
    <span class="legal-label">(e)</span>
    promote the association’s heraldic, vexillological and genealogical heritage;
  </p>
  <p class="legal-item">
    <span class="legal-label">(f)</span>
    grant Arms, by order, in accordance with section 10;
  </p>
  <p class="legal-item">
    <span class="legal-label">(g)</span>
    maintain, manage, and control the application process for people desirous of acquiring or registering Arms;
  </p>
  <p class="legal-item">
    <span class="legal-label">(h)</span>
    publish guidelines on the provision of heraldic services; and
  </p>
  <p class="legal-item">
    <span class="legal-label">(i)</span>
    promote participation in collaborative heraldic, vexillological, or genealogical projects.
  </p>
</div>
