# About Unlisted Thoughts Incorporated

Unlisted Thoughts Incorporated is a collective of people who think too hard about flags, heraldry, and freedom of information—the magical three.

Based in the Great State of NSW.

# History

Unlisted Thoughts began as an informal partnership between two bored high school leavers at the beginning of 2020.
